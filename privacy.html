nest-pi privacy policy

nest-pi is a personal project used only by its owner to control their own Nest thermostat.

  • Thermostat data and Google access tokens obtained through the Smart Device Management API are stored only on the owner's Raspberry Pi on a private home network.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page or the Nest app.
